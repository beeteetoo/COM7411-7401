# Shared source and evidence map — staff only

**Authority order:** validated COM7401/COM7411 specifications → approved assessment brief and local policies → Mbanaso, Abrahams and Okafor (2023), *Research Techniques for Computer Science, Information Systems and Cybersecurity* → supplementary scholarship → legacy slides. Book page numbers below are the **printed** numbers, not PDF page indices. The full PDF remains in `Facilitator Notes (Not for Students)/`. Do not upload it or reproduce its figures in a student pack without a licensed route. Student notes paraphrase concepts in new examples and should cite the book in their references.

The common assessment concerns a **proposal**. Students synthesise published findings and explain how future data *would* be analysed. They are not required to collect participants' data or claim results. LO1 = critical evaluation of approaches; LO2 = ethical, feasible design; LO3 = synthesis, interpretation plan and academic communication.

The published slide master is the ten original six-slide decks in `Shared Module Master/Slides/`, numbered 01–10 to match the student notes. The older decks named in the table are provenance and optional staff references; they have not been copied into the student master because their claims, counts and labels require the corrections below. The new decks use no book figures or verbatim passages.

2. **Correct inherited labels.** The literature deck title says `COM7302`/Research Design; the qualitative and ethics decks also show `COM7302`. The Week 2 AM main title is Research Philosophy although its task is question formulation. In master versions, use `Research Methods` plus topic title; put COM7401/COM7411 only on delivery covers.
3. **Correct conceptual shortcuts.** Remove wording that equates CS with realism or numeric data with a single epistemology. Separate philosophical ontology from domain modelling. State how design-and-creation, experiment, case study, survey or document analysis can generate an answer rather than merely a product.
4. **Relocate overloaded material.** Retain one main task in each live content block. Put alternative review types, extended method lists and AI-platform comparisons in optional notes. Keep essential ethics before Week 3 design and apply it again in the COM7401 Wednesday session.
5. **Credit sources.** The new master decks have compact author/year/printed-page references in the slide footer; facilitator references and adaptation notes are in this map. No book figures or text are copied. Label new scenarios as invented teaching examples. Check any future figure visually and provide a text description when retained.

## Cross-cutting content that needs other sources

The book does not supply a full consent/risk/data-governance lesson, an approved local ethics procedure, detailed mixed-method integration, a full quantitative worked example, a complete thematic-analysis protocol, reflection criteria, current GAI rules or the four oral checks. These must be separately authored and checked against the specifications and assessment brief. The book's APA example and long dissertation/viva framing are illustrative, not assignment instructions.

## Staff preparation and publication check

For each topic, a staff member should confirm the book section, test the worksheet on the worked example, check the slide claim against its source, and verify the output against the correct LO and portfolio criterion. Student-view Moodle checks must show only original notes/tasks and permitted reading links. Staff source files, model responses and the full book PDF remain inaccessible to students.
