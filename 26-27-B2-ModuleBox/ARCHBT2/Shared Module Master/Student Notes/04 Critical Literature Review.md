# 04 — Critical literature review

**Goal:** compare relevant studies so they inform your question and method rather than simply list what each author said. This supports LO1 and the 900-word Critical Literature Review.

For each source, record its question, context, method, evidence, key claim and limitation. Then compare sources: Where do they agree or conflict? Do their methods allow the claims they make? Does a finding transfer to your proposed setting? A good review ends with a reasoned account of what is known, what remains uncertain and how your proposal follows. You may use peer-reviewed computing research and relevant industry or policy evidence, but judge each source according to its purpose and quality.

**Worked example.** Imagine three studies of phishing training. A controlled experiment reports improved test scores; an organisational case study finds staff ignore warnings under time pressure; a design paper proposes a new warning interface without testing it. A critical paragraph would compare what each study can and cannot establish, then identify the evidence your proposal needs. It would not treat the design paper as proof that its interface works.

**Glossary:** *synthesis* — a reasoned conclusion drawn across sources; *critical appraisal* — judging the fit between a study's method, evidence and claim; *grey literature* — research-related material published outside conventional academic channels; *search trail* — recorded search terms and selection decisions.

**Your task:** choose three real, relevant sources. Complete a matrix with citation, claim, method, strength, limitation and relevance to your question. Write a 180-word paragraph that compares at least two sources and ends with the gap your proposed study addresses. Use Harvard consistently for this exercise, as required by the current draft rubric, and check the final published assessment cover; do not copy the book's APA exercise.

**Further reading:** Mbanaso, Abrahams and Okafor (2023), Chapter 5, through a licensed library route if available.
