# Invented transcript for coding practice

These are fictional comments written for this exercise. They are **not** participant data and must not be reported as research findings.

| Speaker | Comment |
|---|---|
| A | “I see so many warnings during the day that I sometimes close one before reading it.” |
| B | “When the message explains why it appeared, I am more willing to stop and check.” |
| C | “I ask a colleague if the alert looks unusual, especially when a client is waiting.” |
| A | “A red banner by itself does not tell me what I should do next.” |
| B | “I trust the warning more if it shows the suspicious address.” |
| C | “Under time pressure I may follow the usual workflow even when the warning interrupts it.” |

**Practice question:** How do staff describe factors that shape their response to a security warning? Try initial codes, compare them, propose a theme and record a rival interpretation.
