# Shared academic master for COM7401 and COM7411

The ten numbered [student notes](Student%20Notes/) and ten matching [six-slide decks](Slides/) are the single content sequence for both module codes. Each note includes a learning goal, original explanation, worked computing example, short task, glossary and individual output. Student notes and master slides may be linked into both Moodle shells. The practice case, abstracts, numerical data and transcript are invented for teaching and must be labelled as such.

The [source map](Facilitator/Source%20Map.md) and [worked examples](Facilitator/Worked%20Examples.md) are **staff only**. The full Mbanaso, Abrahams and Okafor book PDF remains in `Facilitator Notes (Not for Students)/` and is not part of this student master. Refer students to a licensed library copy if available; the existing scanned funnel chapter is not a second core reading.

Use [COM7401 campus and Zoom guides](../Delivery/COM7401/) and [COM7411 online guides](../Delivery/COM7411/) to deliver the same ten outputs. Use the current approved Word assessment brief as the assessment authority. Relative milestones and hour reconciliation are recorded in `Assessment/`.

The [release and pilot checklist](../Assessment/Release%20and%20Pilot%20Checklist.md) records the outstanding assessment, timetable, platform and rehearsal gates. The four-check reference guide in `Assessment/` is **provisional staff material** until its definitions and marking consequences are approved. Do not present it as student guidance.

## Common individual outputs

1. Provisional problem and required evidence.
2. Philosophical position and counter-position.
3. Scoped research question and evidence plan.
4. Three-source evidence matrix and comparative paragraph.
5. Design and ethics rationale.
6. Sampling/data-source/instrument plan.
7. Quantitative method choice and bounded interpretation.
8. Coding/theme example and qualitative method choice.
9. Coherent report draft, revisions and reflection.
10. Timed defence of the locked report against an assigned check.

The first eight outputs are formative building blocks. They are not extra summative assignments. Report and defence requirements, including GAI permissions, come from the approved assessment brief.
