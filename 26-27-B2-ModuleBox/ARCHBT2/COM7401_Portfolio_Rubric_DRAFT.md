# COM7401 Research Methods — Portfolio Marking Rubric

Grade bands (Masters level): **Fail ≤49 / Pass 50–59 / Merit 60–69 / Distinction ≥70**

Total weighting: 100% across five criteria. One band selected per criterion - weighted score sums to the final mark.

Ethics and governance are assessed in the written Methodology section only (Criterion 2), not in the oral defence. The oral defence tests logical coherence between research question and method — a distinct construct from ethical justification. 

---

## Criterion 1 — Critical Evaluation of Methodologies & Philosophical Approaches
**Maps to:** LO1 · **Section:** Critical Literature Review · **Weighting: 25%**

### Sub-indicators
- Range and currency of sources engaged (not just quantity — relevance to the stated topic)
- Evaluative vs descriptive treatment (does the student critique/compare positions, or only summarise them)
- Coherence and explicitness of the stated ontological/epistemological position
- Engagement with counter-positions or alternative paradigms the student did not choose

| Band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| **Fail (≤49)** | Literature is summarised, not evaluated. No stated or identifiable paradigm position. No engagement with alternative approaches. |
| **Pass (50–59)** | Relevant literature present but treatment is mostly descriptive. Paradigm position is stated but not justified, or justified only by assertion. Little or no engagement with alternatives. |
| **Merit (60–69)** | Literature is evaluated, not just described, across most of the section. Paradigm position is stated and justified with reference to the research question. At least one credible alternative paradigm is named and reasoned against. |
| **Distinction (≥70)** | Sustained critical evaluation throughout, including tensions or disagreements within the literature itself. Paradigm position is justified with precision and explicitly connected to what the study needs to produce. Alternative approaches are engaged with seriously, not dismissed. |

## Criterion 2 — Research Design & Ethical/Feasible Proposal Justification
**Maps to:** LO2 · **Section:** Proposed Methodology (incl. governance) · **Weighting: 30%**

### Sub-indicators
- Method is justified against what the research question needs as evidence (not chosen by default or convenience)
- Sampling/data collection strategy is specified and appropriate to the stated method
- Planned analysis approach is named and fits the data the method will produce
- Ethical/governance considerations are specific to this proposal (not generic boilerplate) and proportionate to the actual risk

| Band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| **Fail (≤49)** | Method is asserted, not justified. Sampling/analysis strategy is vague, missing, or mismatched to the method. Ethics section is absent or entirely generic (could apply to any study). |
| **Pass (50–59)** | Method is named and loosely connected to the research question. Sampling and analysis are present but underspecified. Ethics considerations are acknowledged but largely generic, with limited connection to this specific design. |
| **Merit (60–69)** | Method is justified with explicit reference to the evidence the research question requires. Sampling and analysis strategy are specified and internally consistent. Ethics/governance considerations are specific to the proposal and proportionate to its actual risks. |
| **Distinction (≥70)** | Method is rigorously justified with clear awareness of alternatives considered and rejected. Sampling, data collection, and analysis form a coherent, feasible chain. Ethics/governance is fully integrated into the design itself (not appended) and anticipates specific risks with proportionate, well-reasoned mitigations. |

## Criterion 3 — Academic Writing, Structure, Referencing
**Maps to:** IC6 · **Weighting: 15%**

### Sub-indicators
- Adherence to the fixed report structure and word allocation
- Clarity and precision of academic prose
- Correct and consistent referencing (Harvard, as specified)
- Overall coherence — does the report read as one argument, not four disconnected sections

| Band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| **Fail (≤49)** | Structure not followed. Referencing absent, inconsistent, or largely incorrect. Prose is unclear or difficult to follow. |
| **Pass (50–59)** | Structure mostly followed. Referencing present but with recurring errors. Prose is functional but flat or repetitive. |
| **Merit (60–69)** | Structure followed clearly. Referencing is accurate and consistent. Prose is clear, well organised, and sections connect to one another. |
| **Distinction (≥70)** | Structure supports the argument rather than just containing it. Referencing is precise throughout. Prose is professional and precise, and the report reads as a single coherent case rather than four separate essays. |

## Criterion 4 — Oral Defence: Synthesis & Communication
**Maps to:** LO3 · **Format:** 4-minute recorded response to a fixed methodological check · **Weighting: 20%**

**Structural requirements (checklist — complete before applying bands below):**

- [ ] States the research question(s) accurately, as submitted in the final report
- [ ] Names which of the four checks (Build vs Finding / Collapse Test / No-or-Undefined-Word Test / "To…" Test) is being addressed
- [ ] Response is within the time limit (4 minutes, ±30 seconds tolerance)
- [ ] Response directly engages with the named check (not a general restatement of the proposal)

If any structural requirement is unmet, cap the mark at Pass regardless of the quality of reasoning present, and note which item was missing.

### Sub-indicator (once structural requirements are met)
- Accuracy and depth of engagement with the check — does the response demonstrate genuine understanding of why the check matters for this specific proposal, or only that the right words were used

| Band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| **Fail (≤49)** | One or more structural requirements missing. Where present, response misunderstands the check or misrepresents own proposal. |
| **Pass (50–59)** | All structural requirements met. Response addresses the check but only superficially — restates the proposal without genuinely applying the check's logic. |
| **Merit (60–69)** | All structural requirements met. Response correctly applies the check to the student's own proposal and reaches a sound, accurate conclusion. |
| **Distinction (≥70)** | All structural requirements met. Response applies the check with precision, identifies a genuine tension, limitation, or refinement in the student's own design, and communicates this concisely and confidently within time. |

## Criterion 5 — Reflection & Evaluation of Research Quality
**Maps to:** IC8 · **Section:** Reflection · **Weighting: 10%**

### Sub-indicators
- Reflects on the design process itself, not just a restatement of what was proposed
- Identifies specific limitations or trade-offs in the proposed approach
- Addresses how trustworthiness/rigour would be evaluated if the study were carried out (e.g. validity, reliability, credibility, transferability — as appropriate to the paradigm)

| Band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| **Fail (≤49)** | No genuine reflection — restates the proposal or is absent. No engagement with trustworthiness/rigour. |
| **Pass (50–59)** | Some reflection present but generic (could apply to most studies). Trustworthiness is mentioned but not connected to this specific design. |
| **Merit (60–69)** | Reflection engages specifically with this design's process and limitations. Trustworthiness/rigour is addressed with an appropriate, specific criterion for the chosen paradigm. |
| **Distinction (≥70)** | Reflection is sharp and specific, showing genuine evaluative distance from the student's own design. Trustworthiness/rigour discussion is precise and paradigm-appropriate, not generic. |
