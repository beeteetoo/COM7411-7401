# Facilitator guide — COM7401 and COM7411

This is the current entry point for the two parallel Research Methods tracks. The older `Facilitator Guide.md`, `Week *_*/Session Guide.md` and `Zoom Sessions/` guides remain as historical source material; their dates, some slide numbers and assessment format are superseded.

## Shared academic sequence

Both modules use the ten numbered [original student notes](Shared%20Module%20Master/Student%20Notes/) and matching [master slide decks](Shared%20Module%20Master/Slides/), one per teaching topic. Each ends in a specific individual output that builds the same 2,500-word research proposal report and four-minute methodological defence. The [staff source map](Shared%20Module%20Master/Facilitator/Source%20Map.md) gives the corresponding printed pages in Mbanaso, Abrahams and Okafor (2023), names legacy slides/worksheets and notes where another source is essential. The full book PDF and [worked examples](Shared%20Module%20Master/Facilitator/Worked%20Examples.md) are staff-only.

| Week | First common topic | Second common topic | Main portfolio progress |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Research quality and purpose | Philosophy and paradigms | Problem and philosophical position |
| 2 | Funnel and question | Critical literature review | Research question, source matrix and gap |
| 3 | Design and ethics | Sampling and collection | Method and risk/mitigation chain |
| 4 | Quantitative analysis | Qualitative analysis | Analysis plan and report draft in progress |
| 5 | Report integration and reflection | Oral methodological defence | Locked report, assigned check and recording |

### COM7401

Run the two common topics on Monday AM and PM using the [campus scripts](Delivery/COM7401/Campus%20Session%20Guides.md). The additional Wednesday [Zoom workshop](Delivery/COM7401/Wednesday%20Zoom%20Guides.md) consolidates Monday and prepares the next step; it is COM7401-only even though two COM7401 cohort codes appear on the workshop calendar entry. Use the same routine every time: brief retrieval, clear explanation, worked example, guided practice, one main task, individual portfolio transfer and exit check. This predictability supports research-active staff who are newer to teaching. The scripts include exact prompts, expected responses and misconceptions; use the staff key, not improvisation, to standardise delivery.

### COM7411

Run the same first topic live online on Monday and the second on Saturday using the [online scripts](Delivery/COM7411/Online%20Session%20Guides.md). Do not schedule the COM7401 Wednesday Zoom for this code. Each script has live variants for 1–5, 6–24 and 25–50 learners and a low-bandwidth/document fallback. The common individual output stays the same. Post accessible notes and a catch-up route for flexible distributed learners; the catch-up is a learning equivalent, not a new assessment.

## Assessment and timetable controls

Use the agreed formal [assessment alignment](Assessment/Assessment%20Alignment.md) and Word brief as the academic authority. The older Markdown brief's six-to-eight-minute presentation and combined final-day submission are not current. Set relative deadlines separately so each track receives analysis teaching and report feedback before report lock, then receives its assigned check and oral practice before defence submission. Do not give students invented definitions of the four checks; use the approved reference guide once confirmed. Follow the Word brief's distinct written-report and oral-defence GAI rules.

Both specifications require 45 scheduled hours. The [hour ledger](Assessment/Scheduled%20Hours%20Ledger.md) shows that event end times have not yet been provided and the old COM7401 guides explain only 35 hours. Final run-sheet lengths and student-facing schedules require this gate to be resolved.

## Preparing and closing a session

1. Read the mapped book pages, original student note, matching master deck and staff worked example. Use the legacy deck only as a staff reference. Check any slide claim or figure against its source. Do not distribute the full staff book PDF or copied figures.
2. Share the short note and task ahead of time. Arrange a printed/digital alternative, a sample case or dataset, and a private writing route. Make the individual output and success check visible at the opening.
3. Run the track-specific activity, then collect an individual response. Review common misunderstandings and feed them into the next session or the COM7401 Wednesday workshop.
4. Record any accessibility, link, timing or assessment-wording problem in the local preparation log. Do not silently change a marking criterion or deadline in one track.

**Student publication rule:** only the shared original notes, approved assessment, and the correct track's activities should be visible in its Moodle shell. Staff answer keys, full book PDF, source map and archived contradictory guides stay staff-only.
