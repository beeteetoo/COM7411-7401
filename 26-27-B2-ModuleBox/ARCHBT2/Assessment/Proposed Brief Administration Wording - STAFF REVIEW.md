# Proposed assessment administration wording — staff review

This is a proposed **administrative amendment** to `26-27-B2-ModuleBox/COM7401_Assessment_Brief.docx` for the two delivery tracks. It is not an approved replacement brief. Preserve the formal report task, four headings, 2,500-word ±10% limit, four-minute ±30-second recorded defence, assessment 001 weighting, LO wording and authorised GAI permissions. The programme/assessment owner must approve the final wording and rubric before publication.

## Common wording to replace outdated event and submission sentences

> Parts A and B form one research proposal portfolio (assessment 001), completed in two stages. Submit Part A, the written report, through the report upload point by the deadline shown on your module-code cover. When that upload point closes, the report is locked and cannot be revised. Your individual methodological check will then be released through Moodle. Complete Part B, the recorded oral defence, against the check assigned to you and the research question(s) in your locked report. Submit the recording through the linked defence upload point by the deadline shown on your module-code cover. Your defence deadline will follow your track's final practice/support opportunity.

> The check will be one of Build vs Finding, the Collapse Test, the No/Undefined-Word Test, or the “To…” Test. The approved student check guide will define each check and explain how it is assigned. The four-minute ±30-second limit and the Part B requirements in this brief apply to both COM7401 and COM7411.

> For the recorded oral defence, no GAI tool is permitted. This rule applies regardless of whether a student attends the live practice session. Apply the written-report Category B permissions and declaration instructions exactly as specified elsewhere in the approved brief.

These paragraphs replace references to a “Week 4 Thursday Zoom session,” fixed 2026 dates and wording that suggests one simultaneous upload. They do not define the checks, change their marking or authorise a new use of GAI.

All deadlines remain relative in this draft. Insert actual approved dates and times only after the timetable and Moodle workflow are confirmed. Test a student account to ensure the assigned check is invisible before report lock, released to the right learner afterwards, and still accessible before the defence deadline.

## Approval questions

1. What are the authoritative definitions and worked exemplars for all four checks? The separate provisional staff guide offers candidates only.
2. How is one check allocated to each learner, and who performs/records the allocation?
3. Which oral-rubric consequence governs a missing structural item: the draft's Pass cap or its Fail descriptor? Publish one consistent rule and calibrate markers.
4. Does the approved Moodle setup support two linked portfolio upload points with immutable Part A and individual check release? If not, specify the equivalent approved workflow.
