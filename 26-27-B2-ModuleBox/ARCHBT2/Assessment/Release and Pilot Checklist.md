## Pilot tasks and pass evidence

| Task | Campus trial (about 30) | Online small trial (3) | Online large trial (about 50) | Common pass evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ontology debate | Six role-assigned groups, paired debate, individual position card. | Rotate realist, constructivist and challenger; tutor prompts each response. | Poll, rooms of 4–5, shared claim/evidence/counter board, re-poll. | Every learner states a study-specific position, fitting evidence and credible counter-position; no domain-ontology confusion. |
| Design-project question check | Groups mark build task, evaluative question and feasible evidence on the practice case. | Tutor probes each learner's proposed research finding. | Fixed review card in rooms plus individual question submission. | Each learner distinguishes an artefact from the knowledge claim its evaluation could support. |
| Literature critique | Pairs complete matrix from three fictional abstracts, then individual 180-word comparison. | Screen-based joint critique followed by individual paragraph. | Rooms make one comparison row; form captures each learner's paragraph. | Paragraph compares method, strength and limit of at least two sources and justifies a gap for the proposal. |
| Quantitative analysis | Pairs compute both CSV groups; each writes warranted and unwarranted claims. | Each learner calculates one measure and critiques a conclusion. | Shared calculation sheet plus sampled tutor check and individual exit response. | Correct 3/5 vs 4/5 flags and medians 27s vs 20s, with no causal or population overclaim. |
| Qualitative analysis | Pairs code independently; groups propose theme and rival reading. | Tutor-led line-by-line coding with each learner's alternative. | Individual first code, rooms agree two codes/theme, individual exit response. | Quote→code→theme chain is traceable; rival reading and rigour step are stated. |

**Pilot record:** for each size/format, note facilitator, number of participants, duration, completion rate, misunderstood prompt, access issue and exact revision made. A tabletop rehearsal checks whether the scripts are runnable; it does not replace a learner pilot. Release follows the gates above, not merely successful file generation.
