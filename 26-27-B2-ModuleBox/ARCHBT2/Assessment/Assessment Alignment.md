# One assessment master, two delivery covers — staff control sheet

**Current academic authority:** `26-27-B2-ModuleBox/COM7401_Assessment_Brief.docx`, selected for this redesign. It specifies assessment 001 (100% portfolio), a 2,500-word report (±10%, excluding references) with Background, Critical Literature Review, Proposed Methodology and Reflection, then a four-minute (±30 s) recorded oral defence of a fixed methodological check. The report cannot be revised after submission. Keep the same academic task and rubric for COM7401 and COM7411; module covers may differ in code, relative deadline and live support route. The old Markdown brief and legacy Week 5 guides describe a different 6–8 minute, one-day submission and must be marked superseded before students are given instructions.

## Relative sequence for each track

1. Teach both Week 4 analysis topics and provide a report peer-feedback opportunity.
2. Run the Week 5 report integration workshop for that track.
3. Set the **report lock** after that workshop. Confirm the exact date/time in the code-specific cover and Moodle.
4. Allocate and release one of the approved four checks to each student through Moodle **after** the report lock. Do not require COM7411 to join the COM7401 Wednesday session. The Word brief's “Week 4 Thursday Zoom” wording needs an approved administrative correction.
5. Provide a timed *practice* check and recording/access support, then set the defence deadline after the relevant track's final live opportunity.
6. Link report and video under assessment 001 using two upload points or another approved Moodle arrangement that preserves the report lock; confirm the exact platform configuration with the assessment team.

## Content and marking alignment

| Item | Use in both codes |
|---|---|
| LO1 | Critical evaluation of literature, methodology and philosophy, including an alternative position. |
| LO2 | Feasible question→evidence→method→analysis chain and proposal-specific ethics/governance. |
| LO3 | Synthesis of existing findings, planned interpretation, coherent report, reflection and oral communication. Students need not claim newly collected findings. |
| Written report | Four fixed headings, 2,500 words ±10% excluding references; use the referencing style in the final approved brief/rubric. Do not import the book's APA exercise or longer dissertation structure. |
| Oral defence | Four minutes ±30 seconds; submitted question, named assigned check, direct methodological response and bounded conclusion. The book's full viva is an analogy, not the assessed format. |
| GAI | Apply the Word brief's Category B permissions to specified written-report assistance, with critical checking and declaration. No GAI is permitted for the recorded oral defence under that brief. Revise the old “YOU CAN USE CHAT GPT” worksheet before student use. |
