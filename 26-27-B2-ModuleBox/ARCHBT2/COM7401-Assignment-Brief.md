# COM7401 Research Methods — Assignment Brief

## Module Information
- **Module code:** COM7401
- **Module title:** Research Methods
- **Level:** Masters (MRes/MSc)
- **Assessment type:** Portfolio Coursework (Summative, 100% of module mark)
- **Submission deadline:** Session 5 PM (final day of the 5-day block)
- **Submission format:** ONE combined submission per student, consisting of:
  1. A written report (PDF or Word)
  2. A recorded narrated presentation (video file, MP4 recommended)

## Assignment Task

You are required to produce a **research proposal portfolio** consisting of a written report and a recorded oral presentation, in which you critically evaluate relevant research methodologies, design and justify an ethical and feasible research proposal, and communicate your findings clearly.

This assessment maps directly to the module's Learning Outcomes:
- **LO1:** Critically evaluate a range of research methodologies and philosophical approaches relevant to computing and data-oriented disciplines.
- **LO2:** Design and justify an ethical and feasible research proposal informed by critical engagement with relevant literature.
- **LO3:** Synthesise research findings through appropriate analysis, interpretation, and academic communication.

### Part A — Written Report (~2,500 words, excluding references)

Use the following fixed section headings. Suggested word allocations are a guide, not a strict limit per section — the overall 2,500-word target (±10%) applies to the whole report.

1. **Research Background & Context** (~400 words) — *LO1*
   Introduce your research topic/problem area, its relevance to computing/data-oriented disciplines, and your overarching research question(s).

2. **Critical Literature Review** (~900 words) — *LO1*
   Critically evaluate relevant literature and existing methodological/philosophical approaches. Identify the ontological/epistemological position most appropriate to your proposed study and justify it.

3. **Proposed Methodology** (~900 words) — *LO2*
   Design and justify your proposed research methodology (quantitative, qualitative, or mixed methods), including sampling/data collection strategy, planned analysis approach, and **ethical/governance considerations** specific to your proposal (informed by the independent Ethics Pack and the Day3 ethics-in-design checkpoint).

4. **Reflection** (~300 words) — *LO3*
   Reflect on the research design process, the quality/rigour of your proposed approach, and how you would evaluate the trustworthiness of your findings if the study were carried out.

### Part B — Recorded Presentation (6–8 minutes)

A recorded, narrated presentation summarising and defending your proposed research investigation.

- **Recording tool:** OBS (screen/webcam capture)
- **Presentation software:** Student's choice (PowerPoint, Keynote, Canva, etc.)
- **Output format:** MP4 (or a shared streaming link if file size requires it — confirm max file size/hosting method with your tutor)
- **Content:** Summarise your research background, justify your methodological choices, and address how you've considered ethics/governance in your design — treat this as defending your proposal to a critical but supportive audience.

## Marking Scheme — Criteria Rubric

Grade bands (Masters level): **Fail ≤49 / Pass 50–59 / Merit 60–69 / Distinction ≥70**

| # | Criterion | Maps to | Weighting |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Critical evaluation of methodologies & philosophical approaches (Lit Review section) | LO1 | 25% |
| 2 | Research design & ethical/feasible proposal justification (Methodology section) | LO2 | 30% |
| 3 | Academic writing, structure, referencing | IC6 | 15% |
| 4 | Recorded presentation — synthesis & communication | LO3 | 20% |
| 5 | Reflection & evaluation of research quality | IC8 | 10% |

**Total: 100%**

### Indicative band descriptors (per criterion)

- **Fail (≤49):** Limited/absent critical engagement; methodology unjustified or infeasible; ethics not considered; writing/communication unclear or poorly structured.
- **Pass (50–59):** Adequate but largely descriptive engagement; methodology present but weakly justified; ethics acknowledged superficially; writing/communication functional.
- **Merit (60–69):** Good critical engagement with literature and paradigms; methodology well justified and feasible; ethics meaningfully integrated into design; writing/communication clear and well organised.
- **Distinction (≥70):** Excellent, sophisticated critical evaluation; methodology rigorously justified with strong awareness of alternatives; ethics/governance fully integrated and thoughtfully argued; writing/communication is professional, precise, and compelling.
