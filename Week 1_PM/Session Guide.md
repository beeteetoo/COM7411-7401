**Maps to:** LO1; IC1 (foundations of research philosophy and design)
**Portfolio link:** Provisional ontological position — the seed of the Background and Critical Literature Review sections (which must justify one position against at least one alternative).
**Status:** Pass 1 (dual-thread). Deck edited (Pass 2 done: 20 slides). Thread A = COM7401 campus; Thread B = COM7411 online (Saturday morning).

## 1. Core — identical in both threads

### Students leave with
- A working grasp of ontology (realism vs constructivism) applied to CS / DA / CY problems.
- Experience of arguing a position and testing it against a rival one.
- **COM7411 Online:** a first **Stance Card** from the pre-session Philosophical Stance Task, brought to the session. **COM7401 On Campus:** their Stance Card is completed after the session, in Zoom 1.
- A written **provisional ontological position** for their own proposed research (3–4 sentences), kept for Week 2 AM.

### Pre-session prep (both threads — must be done *before* the session)
- **Student cards (release on each module's Moodle site before the session):** `Pre-Session Prep/COM7401 CAMPUS - Pre-Session Card - Week 1 PM.docx` and `Pre-Session Prep/COM7411 ONLINE - Pre-Session Card - Week 1 PM.docx`. They say what to do, in what order and why, with reading guides where there are readings.
- **COM7411 Online:** `Week 1_PM/Pre-Session Prep/COM7411 ONLINE - Philosophical Stance Task.docx` (about 2½ hours; ~55% epistemology; formative, unmarked) — the Week 1 bridge task. The items below are the resources it draws on.
- **COM7401 On Campus:** the items below are the pre-reading *before* the debate. The campus Stance Task comes *after* the session (`Zoom Sessions/Zoom Day1/COM7401 CAMPUS - Philosophical Stance Task (after Week 1, completed in Zoom 1).docx`) and is completed in Zoom 1.
- Lecturer copy for both: `Facilitator Notes (Not for Students)/Lecturer_Copy-Philosophical Stance Task (COM7401 + COM7411).docx`.
- `Pre-Session Prep/OntologyCheatSheet.docx`, `EpistemologyCheatSheet.docx`, `AxologyCheatSheet.docx`, `DoxologyCheatSheet.docx`
- `Pre-Session Prep/Introduction to positivism, interpretivism  critical theory.pdf`
- `Pre-Session Prep/Al-Ababneh-LinkingOntologyEpistemologyandResearchMethodology-2020.pdf`
- Thread B: the task is the Week 1 bridge task, including the forum post (paper classification + epistemology paragraph) and one peer reply.

### Core task
`In-Class Activity/Ontology Debate.docx` — ONE group debate: Realism vs Constructivism on CS/DA/CY scenarios. Lecturer script: `Facilitator Notes (Not for Students)/Lecturer_copy-Ontology Debate.docx`.
The *Integrated Research Philosophy Challenge* (epistemology/axiology/doxology/methodology together) is **not** run in this session — keep it as an optional extension in Facilitator Notes.

### Content delivered live
Ontology: realist vs constructivist views and CS/DA/CY applications · the recap comparison table · how ontology links to later epistemology/methodology choices (signpost only).
Epistemology, axiology and doxology are covered by the pre-session task; do not re-teach — use the students' own work instead.

## 5. Tips and parity check
- ONE activity only in this session — the single biggest fix from the old structure.
- Do not re-lecture epistemology/axiology/doxology; point back to the cheat sheets (campus) or the Stance Task and cheat sheets (online). Watch for "method = stance" errors (see the lecturer copy).
- Keep each student's Stance Card and positioning statement — they feed Week 2 AM and the literature-review position (justify a position against an alternative).
- **Parity check:** both threads produce a positioning statement and a Stance Card (campus in Zoom 1, online before the session); Thread B's debate must keep the two-sides + rebuttal + synthesis structure even when sub-teams are small.
