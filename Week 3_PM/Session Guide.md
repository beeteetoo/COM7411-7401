## 1. Core — identical in both threads

### Students leave with
- A selected and justified **data collection method** (and sampling approach) matched to their research question and design.
- Awareness of primary vs secondary data and the quant/qual contrast.
- A note of any ethics implications of the method chosen (link back to Week 3 AM).

### Pre-session prep (both threads)
- Bring the Week 3 AM Research Design Framework output.
- **Primer written:** `Pre-Session Prep/Sampling and Data Collection Primer.docx` (sampling theory, measurement scales, instrument design). Thread A uses it as a handout; Thread B as the primer or recorded micro-lectures (see Week 3 AM bridge).

### Core task
`In-Class Activity/Data collection Worksheet.docx` — select and justify data collection method(s) for the student's own study. Use ONE activity only (either "Creating the Instrument" or "Data Collection Methods" from the deck, not both).

### Content delivered live
Quant vs qual data · types of data collection (experiments, derived data, observation, simulation/digital twins, surveys) · interviews (structured/semi/unstructured/focus groups; merits and limitations) · instrument design (one summary) · primary vs secondary data (one slide).
Do **not** re-teach ethics clearance here — one-line reminder only.
