## 1. Core — identical in both threads

### Purpose
- Register attendance (engagement record).
- Provide targeted help: rehearsal feedback, recording and upload help, check clarification (within the brief), questions about aligning question and method.
- Make sure every student submits a compliant recording: 4 min ±30 s, states the research question(s) as submitted, names the check, no GAI, no scripts.

### What students bring
- Their submitted report, their assigned check, and any draft recording or rehearsal notes.
- Their laptop/phone, and headset if possible.

### Support categories (the triage list used in both threads)
1. **Rehearsal feedback** — a peer or tutor listens to a defence and comments against the oral criterion.
2. **Recording help** — audio, framing, screen/webcam capture, file export, upload to the Moodle Part 2 link.
3. **Brief or check clarification** — keep to what the brief states; refer other questions to the module leader.
4. **Alignment conversation** — talking through how a method answers the research question.

### Core task
Use `In-Class Activity/Oral Defence Submission Checklist.docx` as the final self-check.

## 5. Tips and parity check
- Keep it light-touch — students are under submission pressure.
- Make registration quick but non-negotiable; it is the engagement record for both threads.
- Prepare a technical contingency: spare devices, alternative recording tool, upload fallback.
- Do not coach scripted delivery or accept AI-written content in rehearsal.
- **Parity check:** both threads use the same triage categories, the same checklist and the same hard rules; outcomes are comparable because attendance and category of help are recorded in both.
