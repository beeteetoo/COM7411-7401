# Week 5_AM — Portfolio Peer-Review & Consolidation Workshop

## Purpose
This session has no equivalent in the old 9-week structure. It is the "glue" session that consolidates Background, Lit Review, Methodology, and Reflection into a coherent portfolio draft before Day5 PM submission, and covers IC6 (academic writing/dissemination), IC7 (referencing/AI tools), and IC8 (reflection/evaluation of research quality).

## In-class task (single, ~90 min)
1. **Bring your draft** (Background + Lit Review + Methodology sections from Day2–Day3 activities).
2. **Pair peer-review** (20 min): swap drafts with a partner; review against the Assignment Brief rubric (see `26-27-B2-ModuleBox/COM7401-Assignment-Brief.md`); note 2 strengths + 2 improvement points per section.
3. **Revision sprint** (30 min): act on peer feedback, focusing on the Methodology and Reflection sections.
4. **Referencing/AI-tools check** (15 min): verify citation formatting and, if AI tools were used in drafting, ensure this is declared per university policy.
5. **Presentation planning** (25 min): outline the structure of the 6–8 min recorded presentation (Day5 PM) based on the finalised report.
