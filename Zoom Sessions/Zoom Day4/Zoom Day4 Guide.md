# Zoom 4 — Report Clinic and Your Check (COM7401 On Campus only)

**When:** Thursday of Week 4 (report due next day). 60 minutes, mass session (chat, polls and forms only; no breakout tasks). COM7411 has no Zoom hour; its equivalent is listed in `Facilitator Guide.md` section 3.
**Deck:** `Zoom 4 - Report Clinic and Your Check (COM7401 On Campus).pptx` (this folder).
**Run sheet source:** `Zoom Facilitator Notes.md`.

## Run sheet
- 25 min: report clinic against the four report criteria (Literature Review 25%, Methodology 30%, Writing 15%, Reflection 10%).
- 20 min: issue of the four checks (release on Moodle during the session), then the defence format.
- 10 min: questions. 5 min: exit ticket (name your check; one sentence on what it will ask).

## Notes
- Prepare the allocation list beforehand with `Facilitator Notes (Not for Students)/Tools/allocate_checks.py` and keep the seed and record file for moderation.
- Release each student's check on Moodle at the issue point; absentees see theirs there.
- Definitions are the working ones in `DRAFT - Four Checks Working Definitions.md`; students get them in `Week 5_AM/Pre-Session Prep/Oral Defence - A Guide to the Four Checks.docx`. Awaiting the module lead's sign-off.
