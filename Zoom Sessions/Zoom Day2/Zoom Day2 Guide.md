## Anti-ghosting note
Make the exit ticket (candidate source submission) a hard prerequisite communicated clearly: "If you don't submit a candidate source today, you'll need to find one live during this afternoon's session, losing critique time." This creates a natural incentive without formal attendance monitoring.
