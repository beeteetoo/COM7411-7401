# Zoom 2 — Research Ethics Core (COM7401 On Campus only)

**When:** Thursday after Week 2. 60 minutes, mass session (chat, polls and forms only; no breakout tasks). COM7411 has no Zoom hour; its equivalent is listed in `Facilitator Guide.md` section 3.
**Deck:** `Zoom 2 - Research Ethics Core (COM7401 On Campus).pptx` (this folder).
**Run sheet source:** `Zoom Facilitator Notes.md`.

## Run sheet
- 40 min: Ethics Core — why ethics matters; five principles; ethics and values; key ethical issues; scientific misconduct; who approves research; what the panel needs to know.
- 15 min: five short cases by poll and chat (deck: Case 1 to Case 5, then "What the cases show"). Ask for the principle at stake, not just ethical or unethical.
- 5 min: exit ticket (name one principle at stake in your own proposal and one risk not considered before today).

## Notes
- Students then complete the **COM7401 Ethics Self-Assessment Worksheet** (`Ethics Pack (Independent Study)/COM7401 CAMPUS - Ethics Self-Assessment Worksheet.docx`) before Week 3 Session 1.
- Record the session for COM7401 absentees only. COM7411 has its own out-of-class Ethics in Research Activity.
- Cases 1 to 5 are adapted from the legacy ethics deck; the answers are on the "What the cases show" slide. The legacy "Assignment Support" 1:1 slide was removed (not viable at scale).
