# Zoom Sessions — Facilitator Notes (Thread A: COM7401 On Campus only)

**Scope:** One-hour Zoom, a couple of days **after** each campus day (Week 1–5). Mass delivery (briefing / demo / clinic / Q&A, cross-cohort up to ~130 students) — never small-group tasks. COM7411 (online) has **no Zoom hour**; the equivalent functions are listed in `Facilitator Guide.md` §3.
**Status:** Pass 1 and Pass 2 done. `Zoom Day2` to `Zoom Day5` hold the Zoom 2 to 5 decks and short guides aligned to this file (Zoom 1 is a worksheet-led session with no deck). The superseded old-order guides are in `Archive - old order/`.

Design principles: each session ends with a short **exit ticket** (poll/form) whose output feeds the next campus day, to counter ghosting; record every session so absentees and Thread B students can use it where noted.

## Zoom 1 — Thursday after Week 1: Philosophical Stance Task, completed live (60 min)
*Full run sheet, extracts and expected answers: `Zoom Day1/Zoom Day1 Guide.md`. Student worksheet: `Zoom Day1/COM7401 CAMPUS - Philosophical Stance Task (after Week 1, completed in Zoom 1).docx`.*
- 5 min: Welcome; purpose; brief questions parked in chat.
- 12 min: Calibration — poll four invented extracts (positivist / interpretivist / pragmatist / survey-with-interpretivist-reading trap).
- 8 min: Values and beliefs audit (axiology + doxology).
- 20 min: Silent writing of the Stance Card.
- 10 min: Share and Q&A (including brief questions).
- 5 min: Exit ticket — topic, paper classification + confidence, epistemology paragraph, one open question (feeds Week 2 AM).
*Online equivalent:* COM7411 does the whole task as the Week 1 bridge, shares in the forum and replies to a peer.

## Zoom 2 — Thursday after Week 2: Ethics Core (60 min)
*Deck: `Zoom Day2/Zoom 2 - Research Ethics Core (COM7401 On Campus).pptx` (rebuilt from the legacy ethics deck). Record for COM7401 absentees only; COM7411 has its own out-of-class Ethics in Research Activity.*
- 40 min: Ethics Core — autonomy, beneficence, non-maleficence, justice, integrity; governance; scientific misconduct.
- 15 min: Case discussion via chat/poll (five cases in the deck; the legacy "Assignment Support" 1:1 slide was replaced by a signpost to the Week 3 AM ethics checkpoint).
- 5 min: Exit ticket (short ethics check). Afterwards students complete the **COM7401 Ethics Self-Assessment Worksheet** (`Ethics Pack (Independent Study)/COM7401 CAMPUS - Ethics Self-Assessment Worksheet.docx`), **required before Week 3 Session 1**.
*Online equivalent:* COM7411 Ethics in Research Activity in the Week 2 bridge (forum cases) + debrief in Week 2 PM.

## Zoom 3 — after Week 3: Analysis and AI/Referencing Tools Demo (60 min)
*Deck: `Zoom Day3/Zoom 3 - Tools for Analysis, Referencing and AI (COM7401 On Campus).pptx`.*
- 40 min: Live demo of quant/qual analysis tools (e.g. SPSS / R / Python; NVivo or manual coding) and referencing/AI tools (Zotero/EndNote; GAI Category B rules and the declaration).
- 15 min: Live Q&A.
- 5 min: Exit ticket — students confirm which tool(s) they plan to use.
*Online equivalent:* Zoom 3 recording (or a standalone recording of the same demo) in the Week 3→4 bridge + 15-min live slice in Week 4 AM.

## Zoom 4 — Thursday of Week 4: Report Clinic + Issue of the Four Checks (60 min)
*Deck: `Zoom Day4/Zoom 4 - Report Clinic and Your Check (COM7401 On Campus).pptx`; report is due the next day (Friday).*
- 25 min: Report clinic — common weaknesses against the marking criteria (critical evaluation, justified design with specific ethics, writing/referencing, reflection).
- 20 min: **Issue of the four checks** — allocation by the agreed mechanism; explain format of Part B (4 min ±30 s, no GAI, no scripts, Moodle Part 2 link). Check definitions are the course team's working definitions (`Facilitator Notes (Not for Students)/DRAFT - Four Checks Working Definitions.md`), pending the module lead's sign-off. Allocation is from a pre-generated list (`Facilitator Notes (Not for Students)/Tools/allocate_checks.py`), released on Moodle.
- 10 min: Q&A.
- 5 min: Exit ticket — confirm the check received.
*Online equivalent:* Week 4 PM Saturday report surgery + live issue (Moodle fallback).

## Zoom 5 — after Week 5: Defence Clinic (60 min)
*Deck: `Zoom Day5/Zoom 5 - Defence Clinic (COM7401 On Campus).pptx`; the deadline is the next day (Friday).*
- 10 min: Rules and the submission checklist.
- 15 min: What strong looks like and the most common problems.
- 25 min: Final Q&A.
- 10 min: Recording tips and final reminder.
- No exit ticket in the run sheet (final session).
*Online equivalent:* Week 5 PM Saturday assignment support.
