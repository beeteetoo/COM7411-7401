## Facilitator Tips
- Keep demos practical and fast-paced — this session works best as "here's the tool, here's it working, here's where to get support" rather than deep tutorials.
- If AI-assisted writing tools are demoed, be explicit and consistent about the university's AI-declaration policy so there's no ambiguity before the Week5_AM check.
