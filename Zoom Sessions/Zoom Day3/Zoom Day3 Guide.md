# Zoom Day3 Guide — Core Ethics Lecture (Special Theme — NOT based on Week3 Pre-Session Prep)

**Duration:** 60 min strictly (all 3 cohorts combined, ~130 students)
**Basis:** Per your decision, this session keeps its special Ethics theme rather than being based on Week3's Pre-Session Prep (which covers Research Design/Data Collection, not ethics). Ethics has no dedicated in-person session, so this Zoom lecture + the Ethics Pack are the primary delivery mechanism for IC5 (Ethics, governance, professional conduct).
**Format constraint:** Mass lecture/discussion via chat/poll — the original deck's small-group case-study debate format won't work at 130 students; convert to a "present case, poll for verdict, reveal answer" format instead.
**Source deck:** `Zoom Sessions/Zoom Day3/Week 9 ethics.pptx` (untouched — see `PPTX Edit Instructions.md` for the one required slide replacement)

## Exit ticket (required)
Students complete the `Ethics Pack (Independent Study)/Ethics Self-Assessment Worksheet.md` and bring their answers to **Week3_AM** (the ethics-in-design checkpoint) — this is a hard prerequisite for that session's checkpoint activity.

## Facilitator Tips
- Do not attempt to run live breakout-room debates for the 5 case studies at 130-student scale — the poll-then-reveal format keeps energy up without needing small groups.
- If time is short, cut the Doxology-Ethics link (Slides 9–11) first — it's the most conceptually dense, least essential-for-application content in the deck.
- Emphasise clearly that "ethics has no separate taught session" — this lecture + the Ethics Pack + the Day3 AM checkpoint together ARE the ethics coverage for this module.
