## 1. Core — identical in both threads

### Students leave with
- A definition of a research problem and where it comes from in CS / DA / CY.
- A **draft research question** (at least three question types written, those fitting their stance), produced with the Funnel Strategy from their own search results.
- A check of each question against their **Stance Card**: which stance does the question assume?
- A confirmed **candidate source** for critical appraisal in Session 2.

### Pre-session prep (both threads — complete before the session)
- **`Pre-Session Prep/Literature Search Task.docx`** (~75 min): build search terms, search two sources, keep a log, triage three sources (anchor, backward, forward), choose a candidate and note one gap. Uses a short recorded **literature searching demo** (outline: `Facilitator Notes (Not for Students)/Recording Outline - Literature Searching Demo.md`) and the Backward/Forward Searching worksheet from the Week 2 Session 2 prep.
- Readings: `How to Choose a Research Topuc Praharaj et-al 2020.pdf`, `READING - a must.pdf`, `Reading - the funnel Method.pdf`.
- **Question-types pre-reading deck** (`To be issues after week 1 PM/Research question Types pptx.pptx`), issued after Week 1 Session 2.
- Bring the **Stance Card** from Week 1 (COM7401: finished in Zoom 1; COM7411: from the Week 1 bridge).
- The Doxology cheat sheet is no longer separate prep for this session — doxology is covered in the Stance Task.

### Core task
`In-Class Activity/Student Worksheet - The funnel Stratergy.docx` — now starts from the student's Search Task sources and gap (Step 2), asks for at least three question types that fit the Stance Card (Step 4), and adds a stance check to the peer review (Step 5). Lecturer script: `Facilitator Notes (Not for Students)/Lecturer_Copy-Applying the Funnel Stratagum.docx`.

### Content delivered live
What a research problem is and why it matters (CS / DA / CY examples) · interdisciplinary nature and the **Lightning Research** Venn activity (15 min max) · question types at a glance (detail is pre-reading) · **how stance shapes the question** (positivist / interpretivist / pragmatist, with wording and CY examples) · the effective-question checklist and examples · the Funnel concept and worked CY example.
Cut from the old deck: the four philosophy recap slides and the duplicate question-type detail.

## 4. Between sessions / Zoom hook
- **Thread A:** the afternoon session critiques the candidate source found in the Search Task (no lunch task). Thursday **Zoom 2** delivers the Ethics Core live; the COM7401 Ethics Self-Assessment is completed after it, before Week 3 Session 1.
- **Thread B:** forum posts (draft question and stance) are read before Saturday; the ethics forum cases are debriefed at the start of Saturday's session. No Zoom hour.

## 5. Tips and parity check
- Protect the 60+ minute Funnel task; it is the most important output of the session.
- A still-too-broad topic at the end is expected; flag it for Week 2 Session 2 and Week 3 AM.
- Watch for stance/question mismatch (for example a causal question from an interpretivist Stance Card) and ask which one should change.
- Keep the Lightning Research activity to its time limit in both threads.
- **Parity check:** every student leaves with a draft question, a stance check and a confirmed candidate source. Thread B students who miss the live session complete the worksheet and forum post before Saturday.
