## 1. Core — identical in both threads

### Students leave with
- A clear picture of the module, the portfolio (report + 4-minute oral defence) and the five-week journey.
- A working definition of research and what makes research "good".
- A completed **Research Impact Case Study Analysis** (group output).
- A broad topic area of interest and a first list of skills/knowledge to develop ("Are you already a researcher?").
- A short written reflection capturing early dissertation ideas.
- **COM7411 Online:** a completed Stance Card (Philosophical Stance Task) before Saturday. **COM7401 On Campus:** the Stance Task follows Week 1 Session 2 and is completed in Zoom 1.

### Pre-session prep (both threads)
- **Student cards (release on each module's Moodle site before the session):** `Pre-Session Prep/COM7401 CAMPUS - Pre-Session Card - Week 1 AM.docx` and `Pre-Session Prep/COM7411 ONLINE - Pre-Session Card - Week 1 AM.docx`. They say what to do, in what order and why, with reading guides where there are readings.
- The skills and topic exercise is now written for students inside the pre-session card (the original tutor-facing activity description moved to `Facilitator Notes (Not for Students)/Tutor Notes - Are you already a researcher (Week 1 AM).docx`).
- Skim the Assessment Brief (`26-27-B2-ModuleBox/0.2 Assessments&Moderation/`) — do not expect full understanding yet.
- **Thread B only:** post a short intro in the Moodle forum (name, background, one topic area); test Zoom audio/video and the shared board.

### Core task
`In-Class Activity/Research Impact Case Study Analysis Worksheet.docx` — analyse a real research case: type of research, qualities of good research, outputs, pathways to impact, evidence of impact. Lecturer key: `Facilitator Notes (Not for Students)/Lecturer_Copy-Research Impact Case Study Analysis.docx`.

### Content delivered live (concept list)
Welcome and teaching team · module timeline and assessment overview · what research is (purposeful, organised endeavour) in CS / IS / CY · types of research (one summary view, not every type) · what makes research good · the research process (overview only) · portfolio structure and the oral-defence format.

### Content moved to prep / cut (decided in Pass 2 for the deck)
Detailed type-by-type slides, repeated "good research" attribute slides, the 10-step process detail. The existing *Research Type Challenge* slide is superseded by the Case Study worksheet.
