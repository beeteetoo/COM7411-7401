## 1. Core — identical in both threads

### Students leave with
- A rough **methodology skeleton**: design type, quant/qual/mixed choice and why.
- A documented **ethics consideration** (participants/consent, risk, data governance) specific to their proposal.
- Awareness of validity/reliability issues in their design.

### Pre-session prep (both threads — *must be completed*)
- **Student cards (release on each module's Moodle site before the session):** `Pre-Session Prep/COM7401 CAMPUS - Pre-Session Card - Week 3 AM.docx` and `Pre-Session Prep/COM7411 ONLINE - Pre-Session Card - Week 3 AM.docx`. They say what to do, in what order and why, with reading guides where there are readings.
- `Pre-Session Prep/Grey Literature.pdf`, `Using_mixed_methods_research_designs_in_health_psy.pdf`
- **Ethics Core completed** (Thread A: Zoom 2 live; Thread B: the Ethics in Research Activity in the Week 2 bridge) and the module-labelled **Ethics Self-Assessment Worksheet** (`Ethics Pack (Independent Study)/COM7401 CAMPUS - Ethics Self-Assessment Worksheet.docx` or the COM7411 ONLINE equivalent) filled in — bring answers.
- Bring the Funnel output and the Lit Review start from Week 2.

### Core task
`In-Class Activity/Research Design Framework - stepwise.docx` — design the student's own approach step by step, plus the ~20-minute ethics checkpoint using the Self-Assessment answers.

### Content delivered live
What research design is (blueprint; RIDA framework: Recreate/Improve/Disprove/Approve) · validity and reliability and the cost of poor design · design types (experimental, comparative, correlational, case study) · qual/quant/mixed overview — **one or two** mixed-methods patterns only (e.g. triangulation, embedded).
Moved to prep: the remaining mixed-methods patterns and case studies.
